I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of Nevada, Reno. My research interests include applied microeconomics, labor economics, insurance markets, public policy, and applied econometrics.

Research

Rent Inflation Shocks and Health Insurance Coverage

This project examines how increases in housing costs affect household health insurance coverage and insurance transitions.

Income Elasticity of Online and Onsite Casino Revenues

Joint work examining consumer responses across online and traditional casino markets.

Canadian Retaliatory Tariffs and California's Wine Industry

Research examining labor-market outcomes associated with the 2025 Canadian retaliatory tariffs affecting California's wine industry.

Teaching

I have taught courses in macroeconomics, microeconomics, and statistics at the University of Nevada, Reno.
